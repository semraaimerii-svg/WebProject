SWAY

SWAY was born from the idea that design is never static. Every concept moves, evolves, and adapts before reaching its final form, swaying between imagination and execution.

The abstract symbol reflects fluidity and balance through its continuous curves, while the diagonal line introduces direction and forward motion. Together, these elements express transformation, growth, and creative progress.

SWAY is not just a logo, but a mindset—one that embraces movement, experimentation, and the natural evolution of ideas.
